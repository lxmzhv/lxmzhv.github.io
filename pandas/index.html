Race your panda from the green side to the golden side of the forest!

📜 How To Play

  • 🎲 Roll the d6 dice to get N movement steps
  • ↗ Choose one of 6 hex directions to move
  • 🌲 Hitting a tree bounces you back (reverses direction)
  • 🐼 Hitting another panda transfers your remaining momentum to them
  • 🏆 First panda to reach the gold side wins!
  • 🟩 Start: left (green) column  →  🟨 Goal: right (gold) column
  • Keyboard: Q/W/A/S/←/→ = choose direction
